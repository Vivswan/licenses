# Changelog

Version history of the Individual and Small Organization License. Each
version is immutable once published; every entry links to its text.

## [1.1.0](./1.1.0/LICENSE.md) - 2026-09-06

Closes five gaps found in a review of the 1.0.0 text. Nothing else
changed.

- Individual License: providing the software or its functionality to
  others, or running it within a product or service offered to others,
  is not licensed. Using it as your own tool, including in work for
  others, and sharing its output stay licensed. A one-person business
  could previously sell the tool as a hosted service.
- Output never includes the software or any part of it, or anything
  that provides its functionality, however produced. A tool that emits
  its own runtime could previously pass copies of itself as output.
- Small-organization income is measured both on the prior tax year and
  on the trailing twelve months ending with the last calendar month
  before the use, and both must stay under the threshold. A company
  formed in mid-December previously qualified all next year on its
  zero-income stub year.
- Patent Defense: a claim by a natural person who controls an
  organization counts as that organization's claim, and a claim by an
  organization an individual controls counts as the individual's. A
  founder could previously sue in their own name while their company
  kept its patent license.
- Violations: notice histories are shared across the group and with the
  natural persons who control it. Each subsidiary previously had its own
  once-per-36-months cure.
